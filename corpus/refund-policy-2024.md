Superseded on 1 January 2026 by the current refund approvals policy. Kept for the record.

Refunds over £250 require approval by a **Director**.
Refunds under £250 can be approved by any team member after checking the order history.
Store credit is offered first; cash refunds only where the customer insists or the law requires it.
